# 🌋 Lavash

**Lavash** is an immersive Augmented Reality game built in **Unity**. 

It reimagines the classic physics-destruction genre by adding a layer of player agency: **You are both the Architect and the Destroyer.** Unlike traditional games where you only knock things down, Lavash empowers you to build your own structures in the real world before switching roles to demolish them with physics-based projectiles.

## 🎮 Gameplay Features
* **Build Your World:** Use 9 unique building blocks to construct fortresses on any flat surface in your room.
* **Physics-Based Destruction:** Switch to gameplay mode and launch Lava Balls to crumble your creation.
* **Scoring System:** Strategically place and hit special "Scoring Meshes" (Crystals, Gold Ingots, Ceramic Jars) to maximize points.
* **Adaptive UI:** Play comfortably in both Portrait and Landscape modes.
* **Game Modes:** Play Solo to beat your high score, or try "Pass-and-Play" Local Multiplayer to challenge a friend.

## 📥 Download & Installation
You can download the latest **Demo version (.apk)** from the [Releases](../../releases) tab of this repository.

1.  Go to the **Releases** section on the right sidebar.
2.  Download the latest `.apk` file.
3.  Install it on your Android device (ensure "Install from Unknown Sources" is enabled).
4.  **Note:** This game requires Camera permissions to detect AR planes.
